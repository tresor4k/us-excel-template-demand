# US Search Demand for Excel & Google Sheets Templates — 1,008 Keywords (2026)

What spreadsheet templates do Americans actually look for? This dataset maps **US search
demand for Excel and Google Sheets templates**: 1,008 template-related keywords with
estimated monthly US search volume, grouped into 16 practical categories.

It was compiled in 2026 by the [TableTemplates](https://tabletemplates.com/) Data & Research
Team while building an open catalog of downloadable spreadsheet templates.

## Headline findings

- **Project management is the biggest category by keyword count** (130 keywords, ~32,200
  combined monthly searches), ahead of CRM & sales KPIs (113) and HR, scheduling & payroll (109).
- **Bookkeeping & accounting punches far above its weight**: only 45 keywords but ~24,200
  monthly searches — the highest demand density of any category. The single biggest keyword
  in the dataset is *excel accounting software* (8,250 searches/month).
- **Evergreen personal-finance demand**: *personal budget template excel* (6,610),
  *excel inventory template* (6,490) and *invoice excel template* (6,460) round out the top four.
- 146 of the 1,008 keywords already have a **free, no-signup implementation** linked in the
  dataset, so researchers can inspect the actual artifact behind the demand.

## Fields

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `keyword` | Template-related search query (US, English) |
| `monthly_search_volume_us` | Estimated monthly US search volume (2026) |
| `category` | One of 16 practical categories |
| `has_free_template` | `yes` if a free, no-signup spreadsheet implementation exists |
| `free_template_url` | Link to the free implementation where one exists |
